I leave my phone in the car.

Out of reach.

Out of temptation.

It feels responsible.

Morning.

The parking lot is quiet.

Something glitters on the ground.

Glass.

Too much glass.

I slow down.

Before I arrive,

Before I even see it,

Before I even know it,

I already know.

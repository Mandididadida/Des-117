I place my phone beside my bed.

Close enough to reach without moving.

Just one glance, I tell myself.

Just one reply.

Just a few minutes.

The screen glows sharply in the dark.

Time loosens its grip.

When I finally look up, morning is already approaching.

I am exhausted.

But sleep never came.

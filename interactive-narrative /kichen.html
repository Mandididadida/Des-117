KITCHEN

I leave my phone in the kitchen.

Far from the bed.

Far from my hands.

The night feels longer at first.

I turn once.

Then again.

Nothing lights up.

No vibrations.

No voices.

Eventually,

sleep finds me.

Deep.

Heavy.

Quiet.

Morning sunlight fills the room.

I wake peacefully.

Then panic.

The alarm rang hours ago.

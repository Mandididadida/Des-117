I leave my phone with my roommate.

Safer this way.

Further away.

I return to bed.

The room feels quieter than usual.

Then a thought appears.

Did I tell her my password?

I replay the conversation.

Again.

And again.

She is already asleep.

I cannot knock.

I cannot check.

The ceiling becomes very interesting tonight.

Sleep slowly backs away.

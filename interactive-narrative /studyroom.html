I place my phone on the desk.

Flat.

Stable.

Safe.

At least it looks that way.

Morning.

I open the door.

Everything lighter than a book is on the floor.

Papers.

Pens.

Memories of order.

My phone lies in the center.

A long crack across the screen.

My cat watches me calmly.

As if nothing happened.
